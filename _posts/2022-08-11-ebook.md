# Make ebook
1. Scan the book in odd and even batches. This is more efficient compared to scanning the book double-sided.
2. Or take pictures of the book and then convert to pdf with ScanTailor Advanced program.
3. pdftk A=forward.pdf B=reverse.pdf shuffle A Bend-1 output collated_pages.pdf
4. Or pdftk A=forward.pdf B=reverse.pdf shuffle A B output collated_pages.pdf (if reverse book is in right order)
5. Use OCR program to generate bookmark textfile separated with tab, arranged similar to markdown format.
6. Insert bookmarks with PdgCntEditor.
